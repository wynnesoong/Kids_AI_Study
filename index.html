PersonalBrain

PersonalBrain is a private, single-user tool. It runs only on its owner's own computer and reads the owner's own Gmail account with read-only access, to organize personal bills and statements in the owner's notes.

It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone else.

PersonalBrain 是個人自用的工具，只在擁有者自己的電腦上執行，以「唯讀」權限讀取擁有者自己的 Gmail，用來整理個人帳單。不對外提供服務，也不收集任何其他人的資料。
